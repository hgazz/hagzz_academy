Two apps. Distinct account journeys.

Give each user the right starting point.

Partner dashboard / web

The main workspace for academy setup, programmes, player records, subscriptions, attendance and collection.

Hagzz Partner / owner

The owner uses the dedicated management account and the features enabled for that account.

Hagzz Partner / guardian

A separate guardian account is activated with the academy. It does not share the owner’s credentials.

Hagzz / customers

The customer app for discovering academies, choosing training and reviewing the available registration steps.

CHECK BEFORE YOU CONTINUE

A player record in the academy dashboard does not automatically create or link a customer-app account. Confirm the required account activation and player linking with the academy.

The guardian has a separate account.

Hagzz Partner / a clear activation and follow-up journey.

01Request activation
02Use personal credentials
03Verify the player
04Review available details

Before first sign-in

Ask the academy to activate the guardian account with the correct contact details and player information.

After signing in

Confirm that the academy and player shown are correct. Report a missing link to registration staff.

When details differ

Use the subscription or receipt reference when asking about a schedule or balance. Do not create duplicate player records.

For a new training choice

Use the Hagzz customer app to discover training. Hagzz Partner is the separate follow-up journey.

CHECK BEFORE YOU CONTINUE

Use the features displayed for your account and version. Do not assume every management-dashboard feature is available to guardians.

Hagzz customer app / discovery before commitment.

01Find the academy
02Choose the programme
03Check the details
04Confirm the available action

Verify the location

Check the academy, sport and branch in Qatar. Review the actual venue and the published training schedule.

Understand the offer

Review age, level, dates, package contents and price where provided. Ask the academy about missing details.

Read demonstration labels

Content marked demo or not bookable is illustrative. It is not a confirmed offer or a request for payment.

Keep the confirmation

Use booking or payment only when available for that programme. Keep the confirmation and transaction reference.

A subscription entered manually by an academy is not guaranteed to appear automatically in the customer app.
